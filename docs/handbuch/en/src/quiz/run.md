## Steps

1. Everyone chooses their team on their phone. Switching is possible until the start.
2. Choose **Start game**. From then on the teams are fixed.
3. Choose a field.
4. The question appears. The phones show the buzzer.
5. Whoever pressed first is shown large on the projector. Half a second after the first press the order is fixed.
6. The **space bar** reveals the answer.
7. Award points per team with **plus** or **minus**. The grid then shows the scores and the played field.
8. The **Z** key undoes the last scoring.
9. After the last question the ranking and the questions for discussion appear.

## Good to know

- **Minus** keeps the question open. The projector then shows the next team.
- **Nobody, close field** ends a question without a score.
- If two teams are less than 50 ms apart, “tied” appears next to them.
- **End game** shows the result at any time. **New game** resets points and fields. Questions and teams stay.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Effect |
|---|---|
| Space | reveal the answer |
| Z | undo the last scoring |
