# Create a pad

<aside class="np-kasten np-ziel" aria-label="What this is about">
<p class="np-kasten-titel">What this is about</p>

- create a pad on the start page
- write in it together and see who wrote what
- enter one's own name
- share the link and save the result
- protect the pad with a password
- manage the pad in another browser too

</aside>

A pad is a text that several people write at the same time. Everyone sees the changes of the others at once. Nobody needs an account.

## Create a pad

<figure class="np-bild">
<img src="../medien/pad-anlegen.png" alt="NextPad start page. Marked are the field Pad name with the entry group-3-interview and the button Open or create." width="1280" height="800" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>A name is enough: if the pad does not exist yet, it is created.</figcaption>
</figure>

1. On the start page, enter a name under **Pad name**, for example `group-3-interview`. Letters, digits, spaces and the characters `- _ . ( ) ,` are allowed, up to 50 characters. The same name means the same pad: whoever enters the same name ends up in the same text.
2. Choose **Open or create**. If the pad exists, it opens. Otherwise NextPad creates a new, empty pad.

Without a name, a pad with a random name is created. The templates under **Start from a template** come with an outline for group work or minutes.

## Write together

<figure class="np-bild">
<img src="../medien/pad-schreiben.png" alt="Pad with the heading Interview guide group 3 and four bullet points. The texts of Lena Hoffmann and Jonas Weber have different backgrounds. Marked are the toolbar, the author colours button, the line by Jonas Weber and the number of participants at the bottom right." width="1280" height="800" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Each person writes in their own colour. The name tag shows where someone is writing.</figcaption>
</figure>

1. The **toolbar** formats the text: lists, indent, undo, font size, colour, alignment and headings under **Style**.
2. The **eye** hides and shows the author colours.
3. What another person writes appears at once in their colour. Here Jonas Weber added the last point.
4. The number at the bottom right shows how many people are in the pad.

## Enter one's own name

<figure class="np-bild">
<img src="../medien/pad-teilnehmende.png" alt="Open list Participants. Marked are the field My name with Lena Hoffmann, the buttons Extend by a year and Delete pad under My pad, and the entry Jonas Weber in the list." width="1280" height="800" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>“Participants” holds one's own name and everyone who is in the pad.</figcaption>
</figure>

1. Tap the number at the bottom right and enter one's own name under **My name**.
2. Whoever created the pad sees **My pad** here: how long it is kept, **Extend by a year** and **Delete pad**.
3. Below are all people in the pad, with their colour.

## Share and save

<figure class="np-bild">
<img src="../medien/pad-teilen.png" alt="Open menu Share and export at the bottom left. Marked are the button Share and export, the entry Copy link and the list of export formats HTML, Markdown, Text, PDF and Print." width="1280" height="800" loading="lazy">
<figcaption>Whoever opens the link writes along. The result can be saved in five formats.</figcaption>
</figure>

1. Open **Share and export** at the bottom left.
2. Choose **Copy link** and pass the link on, for example in the chat of a video conference or in an email to the group. On a phone there is also **Share via device**.
3. Under **Export this pad**, save the text as HTML, Markdown, plain text or PDF, or print it.

## Protect with a password

1. Choose the number at the bottom right. Under **My pad** is the section **Protection**.
2. Enter a **Password** with 6 to 128 characters and choose **Set password**. Passwords that are easy to guess, such as “123456” or the pad name, are rejected. A short phrase or two words with a number work well, such as “red-board-7”.
3. Everyone else enters the password the next time they open the pad. The browser remembers the access for 30 days. A lock next to the number shows that the pad is protected.

**Change password** locks out everyone who knew the old one. Anyone who has the pad open sees a notice and enters the new password after reloading. **Remove password** opens the pad again. Whoever created the pad needs no password.

## Manage in another browser

Extending, deleting and the password are tied to the browser the pad was created in. An owner link extends managing to another browser, for example on the lecture hall PC.

1. Choose the number at the bottom right. Under **My pad** is the section **Owner link**.
2. Choose **Create owner link** and save the link with **Copy**, for example in a note or a draft email. The link appears only this once.
3. Open the link in the other browser and choose **Take over in this browser**. No password is needed for this.

Both browsers then manage the pad. **Create new owner link** or **Revoke owner link** takes managing away again from all browsers that joined through the old link. Whoever has it manages the pad: the link does not belong in the pad or with the group.

## Good to know

- Without a password, anyone who knows the name or the link can read and write. A name with course and date is harder to guess. The password keeps strangers out but does not encrypt the content. Names and other personal data of third parties do not belong in such a pad.
- For courses with fixed groups, the pad as an activity in [Moodle](../moodle/index.html) or [ILIAS](../ilias/index.html) is the better choice. Then only course members get in.
- The pad belongs to the browser it was created in. **My pad**, extending, deleting and protection appear only there. If the data of this browser is cleared, only an owner link saved beforehand helps.
- How long a pad is kept is set by whoever runs NextPad. Opening or editing extends the period.
- **View earlier versions** at the bottom left shows how the text came about. Whoever created the pad can bring back an earlier version.

## Try it yourself

Create a pad with a name of one's own and enter one's own name. Then copy the link, open it in a second browser or on a phone and write a line there. Both windows show the line at once, each in its own colour.

```text
Interview guide group 3
```
