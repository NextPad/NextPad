## Steps

1. On the start page, choose **Idea collection**.
2. Use the **pencil** next to the heading to enter the question of the collection, at most 200 characters.
3. Choose **Save**. The question is shown above the collection.
4. Under **Share, show, save**, choose **Open board**. It shows the collection large for the projector.
5. Phones scan the **QR code** on the board and take part without an account.

Instead of the QR code, **Copy link to take part** also works, for example for the chat of a video conference.

## Good to know

- Only the browser in which the collection was created can moderate. The host should therefore stay in the same browser.
- The board updates itself. It works best in its own window on the projector, with the editing view on your own screen.
- In Moodle and ILIAS courses, the idea collection is also available as an activity. Then only course members can take part, and teachers moderate. [Embed in Moodle](../moodle/index.html) and [Embed in ILIAS](../ilias/index.html) show how.

## Try it yourself

Create a collection with the question from the film and open the board. Then scan the QR code with your own phone and write a first entry.

```text
What should we clarify in the next session?
```
