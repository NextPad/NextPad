## Steps

1. On the phone, enter an entry under **Your idea or question**. No name is asked for.
2. Choose **Submit**.
3. The entry appears at once on the board and on all phones.
4. Agree with the **arrow** to the left of an entry. There is one vote per entry. A second tap takes it back.
5. The board sorts by votes. It shows the six most popular open entries.
6. The host moderates in the **editing view**. Two buttons are shown to the right of each entry.
7. Use the **tick** to **mark** an answered question **as done**.
8. Use the **crossed-out eye** to **hide** what does not fit. Only the host still sees it.
9. Done and hidden entries have lists of their own below the collection. **Reopen** and **Show again** bring them back there.
10. Finally, under **Save**, store the collection as **Markdown** or **CSV for spreadsheets**.

## What the saved file contains

- Entries are sorted by votes. Done entries are marked.
- Hidden entries are left out.
- The CSV file has the columns No, Entry, Votes and Done. In English it is separated by commas.

## Limits

- A collection holds up to 500 entries, one person writes up to 30.
- **Popular** and **New** above the list only change your own sorting, not that of the board.
- **Delete collection** removes all entries and votes for good. Save first.

## Try it yourself

Work with two devices in a collection of your own: write two questions on the phone, vote for one of them on the computer. Then mark a question as done and open the CSV file in a spreadsheet program.
